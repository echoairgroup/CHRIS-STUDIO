04 — JOURNEY

A SIMPLE WAY TO KEEP MOVING

Good work is less about getting everything right on the first try, and more about staying curious enough to improve it.

01

THE SPARK

Notice a possibility.

It might be a problem that could be solved more cleanly, an experience that could be more engaging, or simply an idea that refuses to leave your head.

QUESTION
THE DEFAULT
02

THE BUILD

Give the idea a shape.

Work out what it needs to do, choose a sensible foundation and turn the first version into something tangible. Start small enough to keep moving.

MAKE IT
REAL
03

THE ITERATION

Make it feel right.

Test the details. Fix the awkward parts. Pay attention to the experience, not just whether the feature technically works.

REFINE
THE DETAILS
04

WHAT'S NEXT

Leave room to grow.

Share the work, learn from it and keep space open for the next idea. The best version is often the one you have not made yet.

CURIOUS
