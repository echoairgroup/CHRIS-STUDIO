WHY THIS STUDIO EXISTS

I like the point where an idea becomes something you can open, click, test or explore.

That is what connects the projects on this site. Some are websites, some are tools and others are interactive experiences. They do not all solve the same problem, but they all start with curiosity and a reason to build.

Aviation is a big part of what interests me. Flight simulation, aircraft, airline operations and the software around them have inspired projects such as Echo Air Group, Flightdeck Academy, FlyTech and SKYLINE.

I also enjoy working on things outside aviation. HUMANITY is a geography game, Airport Tycoon is an airport-management game, and the personal dashboards are about making everyday planning a little easier.

Chris Studio brings those ideas together. It is a place to show the work, explain what it does and keep track of what is still taking shape.
