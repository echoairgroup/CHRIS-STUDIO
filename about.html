THE IDEA BEHIND THE WORK

I enjoy turning a question into something real — a website people can explore, a tool that solves a problem, or a world that invites people to play.

My projects tend to start with curiosity. I notice something that could work better, imagine an experience that does not exist yet, or find a topic I want to understand more deeply. Then I start building.

Aviation is a recurring theme in the collection: flight simulation, aircraft, airline operations and the systems around them. That interest has led to projects ranging from cockpit learning tools and live flight information to flight-planning workspaces and a virtual airline.

But the studio is not only about aviation. I also enjoy making geography games, personal productivity tools, website dashboards and interactive environments. The common thread is the experience itself: it should be clear, useful, detailed and enjoyable to use.

Chris Studio is the place where all of that comes together. It is a portfolio, a project index and a record of ideas that are still growing.
