THE PERSON BEHIND THE PROJECTS

C.CHRIS
STUDIO

I like the moment when an idea stops being just an idea and starts becoming something you can click, use, test or explore.

That is what brings the projects on this site together. Some are websites, some are tools, and some are interactive experiences. They do not all serve the same purpose, but they all begin with curiosity and the urge to build.

My interests often lead me towards technology, aviation and the way people interact with digital products. I enjoy figuring out how the pieces fit together — then improving the result until it feels considered and useful.

Chris Studio is where I bring that work together. It is part portfolio, part notebook and part record of what I am learning along the way.
